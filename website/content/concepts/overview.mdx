## How the pieces connect

<div className="flow-line" aria-label="Message delivery model">
  <span>Message contract</span><b>→</b><span>Send or publish</span><b>→</b><span>Topology binding</span><b>→</b><span>Receive endpoint</span><b>→</b><span>Matching consumer</span><b>→</b><span>Settle or fault</span>
</div>

The most important distinction is between an endpoint and a consumer. A receive endpoint owns the queue and receive pipeline. Message bindings decide what can arrive there. Consumer registrations decide which handlers run for each delivery.

## Where compatibility stops being the explanation

Familiarity with MassTransit transfers well, but MyServiceBus documents its own verified behavior and platform parity. Use the interoperability guides when the other framework is actually present on the wire; use these concept pages to understand MyServiceBus itself.
